GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you begin.

Do I need bookkeeping experience?

No. Foundry Membership includes a complete Beginner Track designed to guide you through learning, software certifications (QBO/Xero), and getting setup safely before taking paying clients.

How do the discount codes work?

Type FOUNDER19 or FOUNDER49 in the coupon boxes under the pricing cards to lock in your discounted monthly rate forever.

Can I stay in the community after finishing training?

Yes! You can either stay in the main membership or transition to our $19/mo Alumni Pass to keep total access to Slack, daily Zoom coworking, and community referral networks.

Is this a bookkeeping franchise?

No. Members operate 100% independent businesses and keep all client contracts, brand ownership, and revenue.
