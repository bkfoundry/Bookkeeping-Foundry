GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you begin.

Do I need bookkeeping experience?

No. Starter is designed to help aspiring bookkeepers understand the industry and begin learning. You should build the skills and qualifications needed before serving paying clients.

Can I join the Workspace without training?

Yes. Foundry Workspace is a standalone membership intended for bookkeepers who want community, coworking, and professional connections.

Are the document templates legal advice?

No. Templates are starting points only. Have an appropriate professional review documents for your state and circumstances.

Is this a bookkeeping franchise?

No. Members operate their own independent businesses and keep control of their clients and business revenue.

Is the platform accepting paid members now?

This independently built version is a preview. Checkout and member accounts will be enabled after the necessary services are configured and tested.
